Collection & Quarantine Safeguards
  • Automatic Unique Bag ID (BBMS-F-020, F-021): A unique, collision-proof identifier formatted as BAG-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX is generated at the moment of intake and guaranteed by database constraints.
  • Default Quarantine Status (BBMS-F-026): Newly collected blood units are immediately locked in Quarantined status. They are excluded from available inventory and allocation until all four serology tests pass.
  • Immediate Donor History Update (BBMS-F-019): Recording this collection updates the donor's donation record, enabling profile history displays and interval calculations.
  • Authorized Staff Audit (BBMS-SR-003): The intake session records your staff user credentials ({{ user.username }}) and the authoritative server timestamp.
